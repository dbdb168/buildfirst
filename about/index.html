About

Built by someone who’s been on both sides.

I’m David Beath. For 25 years I worked inside big organisations and the consultancies that advise them. I’ve sold the vision, and I’ve been the one trying to make it real.

Photo to come

Now I build AI tools every day, and I write down what I learn, breakage included, in The AI Cookbook.

BuildFirst exists because of something I kept seeing. Capable leaders, told to run the business and work out AI at the same time, with nobody to show them how. They’d read everything. It still felt hollow, because reading never shows you what AI can do.

You can’t get fluent by reading about it. You build, break it and go again. That’s what we do together.

I work in Claude most days, but I’m tool-agnostic, so we use whatever fits you best. I’m based in Richmond, Virginia, and I teach product innovation as an adjunct at Virginia Commonwealth University.

01

Where the method comes from.

Every part of BuildFirst is something I learned the hard way somewhere else.

Prototype first.

At IDEO, in London, Singapore and Dubai, I learned to make an idea real before arguing about it. At 3Pillar Global, prototype-first selling became a way of winning work.

Experiment at scale.

At DBS Bank in Singapore, I ran innovation management for India’s first digital-only bank, which ran more than a thousand experiments in its first year.

Simplify the work.

At Capital One, I led product managers who brought thousands of workflows down to a couple of hundred, by acting them out before redesigning them.

Design the whole experience.

From the founding team at lastminute.com to new hotel brands in the Middle East, I learned that a brand is what people experience, not only what they’re told.

02

Not a big firm. Not alone, either.

You work with me directly. When a project needs more, I bring in people I trust.

Designers, engineers and specialists in fields like healthcare join when the work calls for them, under the same rules about your data and the same promise that a person makes the call. You’ll always know who is involved, and why.

03

Why it’s called BuildFirst.

Because the build comes first. Before the strategy deck, before the vendor shortlist, before the policy.

Build first, learn fast, repeat. It’s how I coach, how I run a workshop and how I build a system. It’s also how I run this business. How we work says more.
