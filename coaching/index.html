One-to-one coaching for leaders who learn by doing. You build something real in the first session, on your own work.

01

What changed for people who came before you.

  • A technology executive handed his monthly report for the CIO to AI, pulled from his own email and chats.
  • A banking leader used AI as a panel of advisers for a board decision.
  • A technology executive set up three agents of his own, starting from one question: what would you want done for you overnight?
  • A homebuilder’s chief executive now has AI check his models and deal papers first, so he goes into each deal with sharper questions.
02

What happens, session by session.

Two things run side by side. We work on whatever you’re actually trying to solve. And you build something real each time, which is how you learn what AI can and can’t do.

0
Before we start

A short assessment, and a daily briefing.

Ten minutes on what drains your energy, how you like to learn and what you’d build if you could. We set up your tools and switch on a short daily briefing on what’s moving in AI, written for you.

1
Session one

You build something on day one.

Something personal and real, built live, on made-up data so nothing is at risk.

2
Sessions two to six

Your real problems.

Board papers, call preparation, a dashboard you’ve wanted for years. Each session deepens a build or starts a new one, and we back into the theory once you’ve felt why it works.

3
Session seven

Your organisation.

Governance, build or buy, what a sensible rollout looks like.

4
Session eight

On your own.

We review what you’ve built and how you work now, and set you up to keep going without us.

03

Four skills that matter more than prompting.

We teach fluency using the AI Fluency Framework developed by Rick Dakan and Joseph Feller with Anthropic.

Delegation.

Deciding what to hand to AI, what to keep and what to share.

Description.

Saying clearly what you want, with the context it needs.

Discernment.

Judging what comes back: what’s right, what’s off and what’s missing.

Diligence.

Using it responsibly, and owning the result.

04

What you leave with.

  • Your own fluency. Knowing what to hand to AI, how to ask for it, how to judge what comes back, and how to use it responsibly.
  • Three or four tools you built. Small, working, yours.
  • Sharper calls. On tools, vendors, governance and where to invest, made from your own experience.
  • A habit that lasts. Ten minutes a day, and the confidence to ask the machine how to fix it when something doesn’t work.

06

Questions people ask.

Do I have to be technical?

No. Your coach is a strategist and product leader, not an engineer, and learned to build with AI the same way you will.

What’s the time commitment?

Eight sessions of 60 to 90 minutes, weekly or fortnightly, in person or on a call, with ten to twenty minutes of practice in between.

Which tools will we use?

Whatever fits you, starting with the AI tools your company already uses.

What about our data?

We start on made-up data. When real work comes in, we sign an NDA and stay inside the tools and rules your company already has.

Can two of us do it together?

Yes. Two leaders from the same firm can share the programme.
