01

How a build works.

Each step is priced when you reach it. You can stop at any step.

1
Day one

Prototype.

Built on made-up data. You get something working to react to.

2
Two to six weeks

Discovery to prototype.

We work alongside the people who will use it. You get a working prototype, a written build-or-buy view and a plan for a pilot.

3
A few weeks, live

Pilot on real data.

Privacy, security and regulatory work first. A small group uses it on real work. You get measured results against how things were.

4
Launch

Production.

Built and launched for everyone who needs it, in your environment or hosted by us.

5
Three months

Improvement after launch.

We improve it from user feedback once it’s in real use.

02

How we build it.

Context first.

We map your files and write the fact sheets the AI reads before any agent runs.

Tested on your questions.

The people who will use it write real questions, and it has to answer them before launch.

Every answer shows its source.

Down to the page.

A person decides.

The system suggests. Nothing important happens without a check.

Your tools.

We build on the AI tools and systems you already use, whichever they are, in your own environment or hosted by us.

03

Every step ends with a choice.

Whatever you choose, the groundwork is yours to keep.

Keep building

The prototype did what it needed to. We move to a pilot on real data.

Buy instead

A product on the market does it well enough. We’ll tell you which, and what to ask the vendor.

Stop and rethink

The idea needs work. Better to learn that now, while it’s cheap.

04

What we’ve built.

Fortune 500 healthcare technology

Sales leads, twice a day.

An agent checks about 50 state procurement sources and delivers leads to sellers, with what changed and why it matters. Passed the company’s security, legal and insurance reviews. In production.

Fortune 500 insurer

Event emails without the production grind.

One draft becomes a finished, on-brand email inside the company’s CRM, with two human checks before anything goes out.

Special education school

Less paper, problems spotted the same day.

Paper program books become one live record that applies the school’s own rules and suggests the next step. A person decides. In build now.

Insurance brokerage

Growth in one view.

A dashboard built with the chief executive in coaching, then a prototype for finding new prospects.

05

After launch.

Once a build is live, you choose how much to hand to us: run it yourself with our code and documentation, have us maintain it, or have us manage it end to end.
