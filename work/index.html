03

Builds.

Systems that run every day, each one started as a prototype.

Healthcare technology

A procurement watch for sellers.

An agent reads state procurement sources twice a day and tells sellers what changed and why it matters. It went through enterprise security, legal and insurance review, now runs every month and is spreading across product lines.

From paper program books to one up-to-date record.

Teachers record each session once. An AI assistant applies the school’s own rules for the next step and suggests it, but never makes the call. Build or buy weighed honestly, and privacy settled before any real data.

Brand templates, ready-made prompts and an automatic route from first draft to sent email, inside the company’s own systems. A person checks it twice before anything goes out.
