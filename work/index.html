Builds.

Systems that run every day, each one started as a prototype.

Healthcare technology

A procurement watch for sellers.

An agent reads state procurement sources twice a day and tells sellers what changed and why it matters. It went through enterprise security, legal and insurance review, now runs every month and is spreading across product lines.

Special education

From paper program books to a living record.

Session data captured once. A copilot that runs the school’s own decision protocol and suggests the next step, never makes it. Build or buy weighed honestly, and privacy settled before any real data.

Insurance · Investor relations

A creative engine for an annual event.

A design system, a prompt library and an automated email pipeline into the company’s own systems, with two human review points before anything goes out.
