How we look after what we build

More of our clients ask us to run what we build for them.

We build agents and software for our clients. More and more, they ask us to run and maintain what we’ve built for them once it’s live.

Book a 30-minute conversation → What we build
01

The old choice.

For most small and mid-sized businesses there were two options. Buy a SaaS product built for everyone, paying for features you don’t use and working around the ones you need. Or live without the software.

Companies that built their own usually handed the upkeep to a third party, nearshore or offshore. Over time, that cost tends to climb.

02

What’s different now.

AI tools have changed the speed and the cost of this work. We build faster, and intelligent automation takes on much of the checking, fixing and updating that used to need a team. That lets us offer new, more cost-effective ways to build and look after the agents and software a business needs.

03

How it works.

Every build includes three months after launch of improving it from user feedback. After that, you choose:

1

You run it.

We hand over the code and the documentation, at no charge.

2

We maintain it.

We keep it working. Enhancements are priced separately.

3

We manage it end to end.

The product is ours to run, improve and keep working.

04

Who it’s for.

For many owners, operators and chief executives, software made for their business has never been a real option. Now it is. We help each one find the right way to bring AI and other technology into their organisation, and decide how much of it to hand to us.
