One kit, three registers

The people, the props, and the two together.

Everything on the journey page is drawn live by one kit: a true-cube isometric world in metres, ink outlines, paper, and a 45° halftone that only people wear. People are jointed bodies solved from where their hands need to be; props are flat paper greys with real structure. Hover any tile to isolate it; the slider resizes everything.

Props

Furniture and things, each a small drawable with its own depth box so people can sit behind them and reach over them. No halftone here: a halftone shape on the page is always a person.

Together

The seven stops as they are composed on the page, people and props sorted back to front. On the page each one comes alive when the path arrives; here they are all alive at once.
