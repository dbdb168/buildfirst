Builds · About five minutes

Tell us what you want to build.

You spend a few minutes telling us what you need. We spend the time to bring a working prototype to the first conversation. Not a proposal, not a slide: something you can react to.

Step 1 of 8

First, a bit about you.

So we know who we’re talking to.

Step 2 of 8

What world do you work in?

Your industry, the size of the business, the kind of work your team does day to day. It helps us understand the ground before we talk.

Step 3 of 8

No need for technical detail. Describe it the way you would to a colleague. What would this thing do?

Step 4 of 8

Who has this problem?

You? Your team? Your customers? Knowing who feels it helps us build the right thing.

Step 5 of 8

The process, step by step. Where does it start and end? Where does it break or slow down? This is the most useful part: the more you share, the better the prototype we can bring.

Step 6 of 8

Time saved, revenue unlocked, fewer errors, a team freed up. What are you really after?

Step 7 of 8

What are you working with today?

The tools, systems and AI you already use. It tells us what we’d be building on, or replacing. Pick any that apply.

Step 8 of 8

Here’s what we have.

Check it looks right. When you send it, it comes straight to us and we’ll come prepared.

✓

Got it. Now it’s our turn.

You’ve done your part. We’ll read everything you shared and start building. When we meet, we’ll have a prototype for you to react to, not a pitch deck.
